The fifth international workshop on Pomsets and Related Structures will take place at LMF, Paris-Saclay University, on 1 October 2026.

The workshop is associated with the (i)Po(m)set Project, a research project at the crossroads of concurrency theory, automata theory, algebra, and geometry. This fifth edition follows RaPS workshops held in Rennes in April 2024, in Rzeszów in June 2025, in Paris in September 2025, and again in Rzeszów in July 2026.

This edition of RaPS will have the special theme categories for probabilities and concurrency.

Venue

The workshop takes place at LMF, Paris-Saclay University. Please see the LMF site for local information etc.
